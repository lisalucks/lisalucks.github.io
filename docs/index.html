Welcome to my page!

My name is Lisa Lucky! I am 22 years old and currently in my final year at Dublin City University (DCU), pursuing a unique bachelor’s degree that combines my passions for business, language, and data analytics. My focus language is German, and I had the incredible opportunity to study abroad at the Technical University of Munich (TUM) during my third year, which further deepened my love for language and culture.

With four years of experience in customer-facing roles and additional school experience with tools such as Excel, SQL, and Python, I am a strong candidate who is strategic and intentional in my work.
